🔧 Connect this page to the master sheet

This tracker reads three published CSV links from the master Google Sheet. One-time setup by the sheet owner:

  1. Open the master Google Sheet ▸ File ▸ Share ▸ Publish to web.
  2. In the first dropdown pick the tab WebExport_Route; in the second pick Comma-separated values (.csv). Press Publish and copy the link.
  3. Repeat for WebExport_Meta and WebExport_Catches (same dialog, just switch the tab).
  4. Open this HTML file in a text editor, find the CONFIG block at the top of the script, and paste the three links.
  5. Commit the file to the GitHub repo (or just share the file) | done. The page re-reads the sheet on every load and with the ↻ button.

Only the three WebExport tabs are ever published | tier tables and the toggle stay private on the master. The numbers shown here always follow the master's Route Tiering toggle.

Sorting and filtering happen in your browser only | go wild, nothing you click here can ever touch the sheet. Data lags the master by up to ~5 minutes (Google's publish cache).
